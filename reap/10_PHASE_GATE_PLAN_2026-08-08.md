# REAP phase-gate plan

**Dated:** 2026-08-08

**State:** operational plan; not a preregistration and not evidence that a gate has passed

Work proceeds one phase at a time. Parallel agents may work only inside a phase
when their files and decisions do not overlap. Connor is the only merger and the
only person who may initiate paid study calls.

## Development model routing

| Role | Model | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Integration owner | `gpt-5.6-sol`, High | requirements, implementation, conflict resolution, evidence synthesis |
| Independent gate reviewer | `gpt-5.6-sol`, XHigh | scientific, statistical, financial, and final verification |
| Fast repository explorer | `gpt-5.6-terra`, Medium | read-only inventories and broad dependency mapping |
| Bounded mechanical worker | `opencode-go/deepseek-v4-flash` | fixtures, repetitive tests, schemas, docs, deterministic checks |
| Final authority | human / supervisor | merges, methodology approval, preregistration freeze, paid execution |

DeepSeek Flash never receives secrets, uses `--auto`, generates research data, or
approves its own work. Codex reviews its diffs and reruns every claimed check.

## Phase 0 — durable governance and baseline

- Put the non-frozen project laws, REAP planning state, and required observational
  reading under version control using explicit paths.
- Establish Python 3.12 as the local target and one offline test command.
- Record the development-tool exception without weakening the ban on paid study calls.
- Gate: clean checkout has the briefing and laws; offline baseline is reproducible;
  frozen tracked artifacts are byte-identical.

## Phase 1 — close existing workstreams

- B: replace the precomputed 78-row projection test with defensible archive-derived
  acceptance evidence and obtain adversarial review.
- A: implement all eight PR #1 blockers and independently recompute every headline.
- C: remove route-confounded cap probes, add hard live-call bounds, pin the SDK,
  push the branch, and remain dry-run only.
- Gate: A is citable, B is accepted, C is safe for later human smoke execution.

## Phase 2 — integrate grader and analysis

- Human merges accepted B.
- Rebase D, resolve the three overlapping files, and correct the item-level
  transition estimand.
- Run one combined clean-checkout suite and conduct independent statistical review.
- Gate: one reproducible grader+analysis baseline with no unresolved interface or
  estimand mismatch.

## Phase 3 — methodology decisions and preregistration freeze

- Supervisor settles transition aggregation, calibration metric, feasible same-route
  cap grids, effort levels, sample sizes, panel ceilings, and accounting checks.
- Freeze prompt, route, schedule, seed, grader, analysis, and manifest hashes in a
  new REAP preregistration. Never rewrite the Phase-I frozen record.
- Gate: all confirmatory choices are fixed before confirmatory outcomes exist.

## Phase 4 — runner and executable budget gates

- Implement schedule/manifest refusal, explicit effort and `max_tokens`, provider
  pinning, cache identity, zero generation retries, receipt reconciliation,
  append-only ledger entries, dry-run cost simulation, and hard panel ceilings.
- Mutation-test failure paths and independently review accounting logic.
- Gate: every requested safeguard fails closed under its acceptance test.

## Phase 5 — human smoke execution

- Human executes the cheapest Tinker probes and the five-call OpenAI accounting block.
- Reconcile usage, receipts, stop semantics, sample independence, and costs.
- Gate: dated evidence settles every required platform fact; any pre-data change is
  handled before the confirmatory gate opens.

## Phase 6 — confirmatory collection

- Human starts one frozen panel at a time; verify hashes and worst-case exposure
  before each block and reconcile cumulative receipts between blocks.
- Preserve every attempt and stop at the first gate violation.
- Gate: complete immutable raw panels, verified ledger chain, and reconciled spend.

## Phase 7 — frozen analysis and paper production

- Run the frozen analysis unchanged, independently recompute primary endpoints, and
  keep confirmatory, exploratory, observational, and synthetic claims separate.
- Agents may assist with code, checking, tables, and critique; humans own manuscript
  prose and scientific interpretation.
